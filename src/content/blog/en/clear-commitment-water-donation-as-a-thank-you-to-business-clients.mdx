**The festive season is a great time to say thank you. To employees for their commitment. To customers for their trust. This is a big concern for many companies at the end of the year. The medical consulting company eHealth.Business took this chance to combine their thanks to customers with a bigger message – with a generous donation to WeWater**

“We work all year long to optimize healthcare for patients through digitization,” says Nina Sodogé, a consultant at eHealth.Business, “but for some people, health starts with access to clean drinking water.”

As their 2021 thank you to their clients, eHealth.Business decided to support the community of Koguto in Kenya by making a donation to provide clean drinking water there for a year.

“WeWater is a dedicated NGO where we and also all those who support WeWater know that the money we donate arrives transparently, efficiently and also in full exactly there, for whatever purpose it was donated,” says Lina Ziwes, project employee at eHealth.Business.

The committed colleagues are concerned with far more than just doing without merchandise gifts in a one-off campaign. “We want to actively raise awareness and, of course, hope to perhaps convince or motivate one or the other business partner to also support WeWater themselves.”

**We at WeWater also say thank you: For this great idea! And we will report on the implementation and use of the donated filters in the year 2022.**
